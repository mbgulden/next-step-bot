3. **Watch for bitterness.** When you feel invisible, ask yourself: have I been waiting for an invitation, or have I been initiating without one? Bitterness is a sign you've been giving without being asked.
4. **Your emotional wave is not a problem.** It's your decision-making tool. Don't apologize for needing time. The people who matter will wait.
